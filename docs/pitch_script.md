# CascadeIQ — 60-Second Opening Pitch
## Indradhanu PCCOE 2026

*(Designed to be delivered live before the demo begins. It pre-empts the three most common judge questions: "isn't this already done", "why AI not rules", and "what's YOUR novelty".)*

---

**[0:00 - The Hook]**
"Good afternoon, judges. I want to start with a surprising fact: of 324 recent AI early-warning papers, only four actually model hazards as causal chains rather than independent events. 

"India's disaster agencies already forecast single hazards well. But a village with a flood-warning system can still be devastated by a flash flood caused by a landslide 20km upstream. Because their local model only watches local rainfall. 

**[0:20 - The Solution]**
"That's the integration gap we solve. **CascadeIQ is not a competing predictor — it's a connector layer.** 

"We ingest IMD rainfall, GSI landslide maps, and CWC river data, and compute the probability that one hazard triggers the next. 

**[0:35 - The Tech (Physics + AI)]**
"Our novelty is a strict Physics + AI hybrid architecture. The causal structure of the cascade is defined by pure physics equations like Caine’s threshold and Korup’s blockage ratio. But the probabilities running through that structure are calibrated by a pgmpy Bayesian Network. We don't ask AI to invent physical relationships it wasn't shown.

**[0:50 - The Impact]**
"We then overlay this on OpenStreetMap infrastructure to compute an Impact Score, generating a tiered, human-in-the-loop alert. 

"To prove this works, let us show you our backtest on the Sikkim 2023 disaster."
